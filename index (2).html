Our Story

Five decades of
ink & honour

What began in 1974 as a small press in the heart of Howrah has grown into one of West Bengal's most trusted printing establishments. Sanguine Printing Works has been passed through three generations — each chapter adding depth to a story written in ink.

We believe in the permanence of print. In an age of screens, there is still something irreplaceable about a well-crafted letterhead, a perfectly pressed business card, or a professionally bound brochure in your hands.

Every job we take — large or small — carries the same promise: precision, care, and family pride.

How It Works

Brief to finished print

01

02
